    Two Coffees and a Beer

    It is midnight in San Francisco, 2017. One engineer has an idea for a clock that blockchains could share. He has until 4 a.m. to get it into the notebook.

    • You run the drinks. Coffee keeps him awake. Beer keeps him loose.
    • Hold both gauges inside the lit band. Ideas only arrive there. The coffee band climbs as he tires.
    • Too much coffee and he rewrites everything. Too much beer and he sleeps.

    One minute. Keyboard: C for coffee, B for beer.
